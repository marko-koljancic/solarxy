Solarxy.
The Solarxy roadmap

What shipped, and what comes next.

Solarxy is a node-based parametric modeler and an inspection, validation, and review tool on one Rust core across desktop, CLI, and the web. This page is the map: the architecture, every shipped release, the release program to 1.0, and every roadmap card with the release it ships in or the reason it does not have one yet.

01 / The state of play

Where the product stands.

The direction is set: grow toward the breadth of a mature web creative engine, in procedural modeling, visual programming, interactivity, animation, and rendering, sequenced realistically. The distance is a heading, not a promise, and this page says per item what is shipped, what is committed, and what is exploratory.

05 / The road to 1.0

Five rungs to the Horizon.

The near-term view: what ships between here and 1.0. 1.0 is a maturity bar, not a feature bar: stable, documented, embeddable, and publishable, explicitly not complete. Capability keeps shipping as 1.x, and the full numbered program is the next section.

06 / The milestone program

Thirteen releases, and every card has a home.

Every roadmap card carries exactly one disposition: shipped, scheduled to a named release, backlog with the trigger that would schedule it, deferred with a rationale, or won't-do with a reason. Shipped, committed, and exploratory work are labeled as what they are, and the labels never blur. Horizon is stated in releases, not dates.

Click any card id to open that card in the explorer further down the page.
